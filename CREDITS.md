# VELLUM credits

## Music

Both music tracks are distributed under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication. Attribution is not required, but the creators are credited here with thanks.

- **Scriptorium:** “Cathedral in the forest (ambient loop)” by congusbongus — [OpenGameArt source](https://opengameart.org/content/cathedral-in-the-forest-ambient-loop)
- **Rotting Orchard:** “Insistent: background loop” by yd — [OpenGameArt source](https://opengameart.org/content/insistent-background-loop)

All weapon, creature, interface, page, impact, and Warden sound effects are synthesized at runtime specifically for VELLUM. No external sound-effect samples are used.
